# recheck:ux — new-player walkthrough of the shipped code, land → connect → class → spawn → gate → muster → passage → fight → shoot → hit → die → respawn → win

## Verdict

NOT CLEAR — but much closer than the last two reports. "I can always see my character" is essentially closed: the camera is gone and `viewport.ts` proves containment for every reachable position, with one 100-150 ms hole at the gate passage where R3 unmounts the local knight before the veil covers it. "Space produces visible feedback" is closed at the input layer — all four chain gates mirrored, a practice arrow in every dead window — but two things keep the user's own experience of it alive: the game never once tells anyone that Space is the fire key or that click-drag aims (the only control copy in the app is one line that disappears when you reach the gate), and the telemetry panel, open by default with no `pointer-events: none`, eats every click in a 280 px column down the right of the screen. Those two are the first things to fix; both are small. Beyond the headlines, the boss's unclipped lower hitboxes make 14.8 % of waiting-room practice shots spark gold on empty stone, a sealed-vent absorb draws a hit-coloured spark with no number from 20.4 % of pit stands, and a player who idles through a whole match in the lobby ends stranded with no result card and no button.

Verified `Shot` is mounted in both rooms (Arena.tsx row 13, unconditional) and its R3 zone gate passes for a same-zone local shot.
- The three copies of the shot cooldown are down to one. `Hud.tsx:56` imports `shotAllowed` from `controls.ts`; both modules carry dev self-checks tying it to `CLASS_PERIOD_MS` through `ticksFor`, including the archer row. The 600 ms-early SHOT READY pill is genuinely gone.
- The camera is deleted, not disabled, and the replacement carries the proof. `viewport.ts`'s self-check sweeps every walkable tile of each room's own zone band (`player.rs::zone_bounds`) plus 48 units of headroom against the room rect, and sweeps the fit over aspect 0.05..6.0 for containment and equal aspect. "A player outside the window is invisible with no error anywhere" is now structurally impossible — except at the passage frame noted above.
- Three independent self-location cues, and they survive occlusion: a never-clipped chevron above the head drawn even while dead (Knight.tsx:676), a two-tone ground ring at 18x7 (Knight.tsx:583), and the dilated halo. `knights.gen.ts` really does carry `k{0,1,2}-halo` and `-halo-fallen` alongside rest/contactL/contactR/fallen/sil, so `HAS_HALO` is true and no `<use>` dangles.

## Still open

- [HIGH] The telemetry panel is OPEN BY DEFAULT and swallows every click in a ~280 px column down the right of the screen. /home/anshtyagi/Documents/pixel-artgame/app/src/ui/DevPanel.tsx:258 is `useState(true)`; /home/anshtyagi/Documents/pixel-artgame/app/src/styles.css:845 gives `.dev` `position:fixed; right:12px; z-index:40; width:268px; max-height:calc(100vh - 24px)` and NO `pointer-events:none` (the only two `pointer-events` rules in the file are `.stage::after` and `.hud`). `controls.ts::attachControls` binds `pointerdown` to `cfg.surface` = `#stage`, and `.dev` is a sibling `<aside>` in `.shell`, not a descendant — so a pointer-down inside that column never reaches the aim path at all. No arrow, no send, no message. At 1920x1080 that is 14.6 % of the width and ~65 % of the height. This is the exact shape of the user's complaint: click to shoot, nothing happens, no error anywhere. Fix is the pattern `.hud` already uses: `pointer-events:none` on `.dev` with `auto` on `.dev-head` and its two buttons.
- [HIGH] Nothing in the game ever tells the player that Space fires or that click-drag aims. grep over /home/anshtyagi/Documents/pixel-artgame/app/src/{screens,ui,render,App.tsx} finds exactly one control instruction in player-facing copy: `Gate.tsx:111` "WASD or the arrow keys" — and it is inside `GatePrompt`'s `gap !== 0` branch, so it vanishes the moment you stand on the gate and never returns. `hud-bc` is mounted only by `screens/Lobby.tsx`, so the arena screen carries NO instruction cluster at all, against spec §9.2's eight clusters ("BC one line of instruction").
